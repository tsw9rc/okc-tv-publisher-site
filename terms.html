Authorized use and service conditions

Service terms

OKC TV Publisher is a private church staff tool for maintaining a Google Photos display album. It is not a public photo-upload service.

Who may use it

Use this feature only with the owner’s authorization and an approved tablet connection. Keep publishing keys and account credentials private. Do not give another person access to the connection without the owner’s permission.

Use only images you are authorized to send to the church’s Google account and display to the intended audience. Staff are responsible for reviewing the selected photos, frames, and display dates before approval.

What enabling publishing does

Connect and enable checks the configured account and album, then enables automatic tablet updates. Eligible previously approved exports can be migrated into the approved publishing set, so the first sync may include existing approved photos as well as new approvals.

The publisher accepts approved landscape JPEGs within its size and manifest limits. It checks the image’s dimensions and rotation; a filename alone does not establish landscape orientation. Received submissions and unapproved photos do not automatically become approved for this album.

Images are sent to the API-created OKC TV — Automatic Landscape album. Google Photos stores API uploads at original quality, using the owner’s Google account storage. Keep this album for publisher-managed images and review the destination before enabling a connection.

Revisions, dates, and retained images

An edit to an approved photo can publish as a new revision. The app confirms the replacement in the managed album before removing its previous revision from that album. Previous uploaded revisions remain in the Google Photos library unless the account owner removes them separately.

Hidden, deleted, portrait, and expired entries leave the managed album after their relevant update is successfully processed. Community Day is scheduled for October 1–December 20 inclusive each year, using America/New_York dates. Other entries follow staff-selected rules.

The tablet prepares a 400-day schedule, and the server checks it every 15 minutes. Scheduled checks and network operations may be delayed. If the saved horizon expires, the service reports that it needs a tablet refresh and withdraws expired managed membership.

Pausing tablet updates does not stop the server’s saved seasonal schedule. Existing library images remain. To stop scheduled cloud changes as well, the owner must disable the server’s clock trigger or revoke its Google access.

Check completion on the actual devices

Network access, Google authorization, available account storage, API quotas, and the server deployment affect publishing. A pending or attention status means that further action or a retry may be needed. Confirm the album result instead of treating a button press as proof of completion.

TV playback depends on the photo viewer installed on that TV and its support for selecting the managed album. It must be checked on the actual TV. The tablet’s screensaver operates independently; this publisher does not synchronize the exact photo or transition time across screens.

Google services and privacy

The account owner controls Google authorization and can revoke it. Google Photos and Apps Script remain subject to their applicable service conditions, including Google’s Terms of Service. Read the privacy notice for the publisher’s data handling, storage, and removal behavior.

Support

Contact brotherdonte.okc@gmail.com about access, incorrect display dates, publishing failures, or these terms. Include the visible status and a description of the issue; do not send connection keys or account credentials.
