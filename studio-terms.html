Photo studio terms

Use the OKC photo studio to prepare photos you are authorized to share with the O?Kelly Chapel community.

Permission and review

Before sending, confirm that you have permission to share the photos and allow their use on the church?s photo displays. For children pictured, obtain permission from their parent or guardian. Staff review every submission before display; a chosen frame or suggested occasion is a request, and staff make the final decision.

Your photo copies

Crop, zoom, orientation, and naming tools prepare copies in your browser. Keep your originals. Review the prepared photos, frame preference, occasion, and season before sending. An upload can fail or remain incomplete because of a network interruption, file problem, or service limit. Keep the page open until it shows a real confirmation; a frame preview alone is not a submission.

Access and appropriate use

Use a current church invitation link or the tablet?s daily code. Share only photos appropriate for the intended church audience. Do not try to bypass access checks, submit another person?s credentials, or use the service to distribute unwanted material. Contact staff if your code or invitation no longer works.

Displays and availability

Member submissions go to staff review, not directly to public screens. Community Day?s approved display window is October 1 through December 20 each year. The tablet and a compatible TV viewer can run independently; they do not necessarily show the same photo at the same instant. TV publishing needs its separate connection and an actual-device check.

Privacy and help

Read the photo studio privacy notice for submission, storage, and removal details. For assistance, contact brotherdonte.okc@gmail.com. Include a confirmation reference when available; never send account passwords or publishing keys.
