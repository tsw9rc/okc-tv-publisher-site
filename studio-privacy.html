Photo studio privacy

The photo studio helps members prepare photos and send them to O?Kelly Chapel staff for review. The public website does not display submitted member photos.

Before you send

The studio opens in the church?s existing Google-hosted upload service. You may need today?s code from the tablet, or a current church invitation link. Selecting, naming, cropping, and previewing photos happens in your browser. The editor works on copies; keep the originals on your device. Closing the page can lose unsent selections and edits.

What you submit

After you review the batch and confirm permission to share, the studio sends the prepared JPEG copies, optional sender name, photo names, occasion, season, frame preference, and crop information to the church?s Google Drive inbox. It records submission identifiers and times so staff can match photos and retry incomplete batches. A crop submitted through the editor is a prepared copy; the service does not promise to store your uncropped phone original.

Staff review and notifications

When a batch finishes, the service sends a notification to brotherdonte.okc@gmail.com with the photos and assessment choices. The church tablet imports submissions into Received for staff review. Staff decide the final crop, frame, category, and display dates before adding a photo to the church display. Custom occasion and season suggestions remain pending until reviewed.

Storage and removal

Google Drive keeps the submitted copies; importing them to the tablet moves the Drive copies into the imported folder rather than deleting them. The tablet retains received photos and their submission metadata for staff processing. There is no fixed automatic deletion period for every submission. Contact brotherdonte.okc@gmail.com with the submission date, photo name or description, and confirmation reference to request correction or removal. Staff handle those requests; these public pages cannot delete submitted images.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the public information site and records visitors? IP addresses for security. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, sign-in form, or member-photo collection form. Starting a submission opens Google?s service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy.

TV album publishing

Staff-approved landscape publishing is a separate feature. Read the OKC TV Publisher privacy notice for its Google Photos data handling. Sending a member submission does not automatically add it to the TV album.
